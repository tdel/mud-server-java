# Ability Scores, Checks & Saving Throws

Source: [D&D 5e SRD 5.1](https://5thsrd.org/rules/abilities/) (CC-BY-4.0, Wizards of the Coast)

## The six abilities

| Ability | Measures |
|---|---|
| Strength | physical power |
| Dexterity | agility |
| Constitution | endurance |
| Intelligence | reasoning and memory |
| Wisdom | perception and insight |
| Charisma | force of personality |

A score of 10–11 is the human average. Adventurers typically exceed this; unmodified human maximum is 18 (20 for adventurers via advancement, 30 for supernatural beings).

## Modifiers

Each ability score maps to a modifier from -5 (score 1) to +10 (score 30), via the standard formula `floor((score - 10) / 2)`. The three main d20 rolls of the game — **ability check**, **saving throw**, **attack roll** — all add the relevant ability modifier to a d20 roll and compare against a target number.

## Ability checks

- The GM picks the relevant ability, sets a Difficulty Class (DC), the player rolls `1d20 + ability modifier` (+ proficiency bonus if a matching skill applies).
- Typical DCs: very easy 5, easy 10, medium 15, hard 20, very hard 25, nearly impossible 30.
- **Contests**: both sides roll, higher total wins; a tie leaves the situation unchanged.
- **Skills**: 18 skills tied to the five non-attack-oriented abilities (Strength: Athletics; Dexterity: Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth; Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma: five each). Proficiency in a skill adds the proficiency bonus to checks using it. The GM may allow atypical ability/skill pairings.
- **Passive checks**: no roll — `10 + all normally-applying modifiers`, ±5 for advantage/disadvantage. Used for things like passive Perception.
- **Group/teamwork checks**: a leader can check with advantage if helped by another character; for a group check, the group succeeds if at least half the members succeed.

## Saving throws

- Resist a spell, trap, poison, disease, or similar threat: `1d20 + relevant ability modifier` (+ proficiency bonus if proficient in that save).
- Every class grants proficiency in at least two saving throws.
- The DC is set by whatever effect triggers the save (e.g. a spell's save DC = `8 + proficiency bonus + casting ability modifier`).
- Success usually means no harm or reduced harm; the triggering effect defines the exact outcome.

## Advantage and disadvantage

- Roll 2d20, keep the higher (advantage) or the lower (disadvantage).
- Multiple sources of advantage (or of disadvantage) never stack beyond a single extra d20.
- Advantage and disadvantage on the same roll cancel out — roll a single d20.
- A reroll granted while having advantage/disadvantage only rerolls one of the two dice (chosen by the player).
- Sources: class/racial features, spells, inspiration, or GM ruling on circumstances.

## Proficiency bonus

- Added to ability checks, saving throws, and attack rolls when the character is proficient.
- Never added more than once to the same roll, even if multiple rules would suggest it applies twice — and never multiplied/divided more than once.
- Scales with character level (all classes share this progression):

| Level | 1–4 | 5–8 | 9–12 | 13–16 | 17–20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proficiency bonus | +2 | +3 | +4 | +5 | +6 |

See [leveling-xp.md](leveling-xp.md) for the full level progression.

## Inspiration

- Awarded by the GM for playing out personality traits, flaws, or bonds in a compelling way. Binary — a character either has it or doesn't (no stacking).
- Spend it to gain advantage on one attack roll, saving throw, or ability check, or give it to another player for good roleplaying/clever play.
